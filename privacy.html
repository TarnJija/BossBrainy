Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

BossBrainy is a personal tool used by one person (its owner) on their own computer. No one else uses it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, BossBrainy uses read-only access to the owner's Gmail account (gmail.readonly) to find e-mails that contain the owner's bank and investment statements and to download those statement files.

How the data is used and stored

  • Statements are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own self-hosted finance app on their private network.
  • The data is used only to keep the owner's own finance records. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • The owner may use an AI assistant on their own computer to help summarize their own records.
  • Access credentials are kept in the operating system's secure keychain.

Google user data

BossBrainy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
